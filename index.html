How this is worked out

  • Daily gas readings come from your smart meter via the Octopus API. m³ readings are converted to kWh the same way your bill does: m³ × 1.02264 × calorific value ÷ 3.6.
  • Daily mean temperatures for your postcode come from Open-Meteo's historical weather archive. A heating degree day is how far that day's mean fell below the base temperature (15.5°C by default).
  • For each boiler, a straight line is fitted through daily gas use against degree days. The slope is heating gas per degree day and the intercept is the warm-day baseline. Days with no reading or no weather data are skipped, as are the days either side of the install.
  • "Same weather" annual figures run both lines over the most recent 12 months of weather, so the only difference between them is the boiler.
  • Anything else that changed at the same time (thermostat settings, people at home, insulation, a new hot water routine) will show up as part of the difference.
